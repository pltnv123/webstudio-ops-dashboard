# WebStudio Visual Sourcing Engine v34

## Pipeline
1. Business category research: industry norms, trust elements, forbidden claims, premium direction.
2. Client asset discovery: website, socials, logo, photos, docs, certificates, cases, reviews.
3. Competitor/inspiration scan: 3–5 best references, adapt not copy.
4. Asset classification: real_client_asset, official_reference, licensed_stock, generated_concept, planned_shoot, placeholder, forbidden_unverified.
5. Asset registry: usage, license/status, approvals, replacements.
6. Shotlist: hero, team, service, process, proof, cases, interior, docs, social/video.

## Medical/dental guardrails
No fake doctors, fake certificates, fake before/after, fake guarantees. Concept visuals must be labeled. Real publication requires approval.
