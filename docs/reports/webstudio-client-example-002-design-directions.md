# Client Example #002 Design Directions

Updated: 2026-05-24T21:26:06Z

## Concept A — Quiet Luxury Clinic
Warm ivory canvas, deep espresso text, champagne accent, editorial doctor cards, premium whitespace. Best for implant/vinyl high-ticket trust.

## Concept B — Clinical Precision
White porcelain, cool grey, structured diagnostics timeline, technology-forward but not cold. Best for surgical confidence.

## Concept C — Magazine Smile Studio
Editorial split layouts, large typography, calm portraits, case-study storytelling. Best for veneers/aesthetic dentistry.

## Recommended winner
Concept A as primary, with Concept B diagnostic modules.
