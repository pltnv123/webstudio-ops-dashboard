## Visual pillars
1. Спокойное доверие вместо медицинской агрессии.
2. Дорогая editorial-композиция: асимметрия, воздух, крупная типографика, мало цветов.
3. Реальные proof assets: врачи, документы, процесс, интерьер.
4. Клиническая чистота без холодной generic-blue стерильности.
5. Motion как режиссура внимания, не декоративный шум.

## Anti-template rules
- no generic stock doctors, no fake smiles, no fake ratings/logos/testimonials.
- no miracle before/after, no guarantees, no aggressive discount banners.
- no blue/purple SaaS gradients for clinics.

## Typography
Serif headline for premium calm authority + humanist sans for navigation and forms.

## Palette logic
Warm ivory canvas, graphite ink, muted champagne/stone accent, clinical off-white surfaces.

## Imagery rules
Real shoot > generated concept > abstract editorial placeholder. Generated assets must be labeled as concept visuals until replaced.

## Trust/proof blocks
Doctor bio, certificate wall, case story with constraints, consultation process, legal proof note.

## Premium CTA
Primary: calm solid graphite/champagne button. Secondary: Telegram outline. Avoid alarm colors.
