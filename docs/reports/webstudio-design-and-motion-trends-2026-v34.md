# Design and Motion Trends 2026 — WebStudio v34

## Adopt
- Editorial luxury: whitespace, typography, calm hierarchy.
- Trust-first healthcare conversion: doctors, documents, reviews, insurance/financing, first-visit path.
- Premium micro-motion: small translate/opacity, card reveals, CTA feedback.
- AI-citable structured content: FAQs, service details, local geography, proof policy.
- Accessibility-first: reduced motion, focus states, contrast, pause controls.
- Performance budgets: static poster before video, lazy video, lightweight assets.

## Use carefully
- WebGL / R3F hero: use for product/architecture/AI/beauty; not dental default.
- ScrollTrigger: reveal only; avoid pinning/hijack in healthcare.
- Video background: short, muted, poster, pause, no reduced-motion load.

## Avoid
- fake doctors/certificates/reviews;
- medical guarantees;
- huge autoplay hero videos;
- generic stock visuals;
- experimental nav in conversion pages;
- blue/purple AI gradients as premium default.

## Dental conversion rules
- Show location, consultation CTA, Telegram route, services, doctors, documents, process.
- Use cautious language: treatment plan after diagnosis, results vary, candidacy depends on consultation.
- Keep sensitive forms privacy-conscious and free from third-party ad pixels by default.
