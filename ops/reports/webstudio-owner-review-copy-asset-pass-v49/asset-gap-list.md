# Asset Gap List — V4.9

## Real photos needed
- Hero photos per client/industry.
- Service/process imagery.
- Team/headshots/location/product images where relevant.
- Before/after screenshots for portfolio proof.

## Logos / brand assets needed
- Client logo, favicon, color palette, typography preference.
- Approved brand usage rules.
- Partner/customer logos only with permission.

## Testimonials / proof needed
- Real reviews/testimonials with consent.
- Measurable proof only if verified.
- Case-study screenshots and delivery evidence.

## Compliance review needed
- Medical, legal, financial, wellness, claims-heavy copy.
- Privacy wording before collecting real leads.
- Cookie/analytics/contact-form policy before public production launch.

## Before/after/project proof needed
- Screenshots of actual shipped sites.
- GitHub/QA/deploy proof can remain operational, but commercial proof needs client-readable artifacts.
