# V6.6 Proof policy

Allowed proof:
- approved screenshots from delivered public pages;
- client-supplied logos with written permission;
- signed-off testimonial text from named client;
- verified analytics or exported metrics with date/source;
- QA reports, route smoke checks, handoff artifacts and public links;
- sanitized process screenshots with private data removed.

Forbidden:
- fake testimonials;
- fake metrics, ROI, conversion lifts, traffic, revenue, rankings;
- fake logos or implied client relationship;
- fabricated credentials/certifications;
- medical/legal overclaims;
- private client data.

If proof is missing, status is `BLOCKED_NO_PROOF` or `DEMO_PLACEHOLDER`, never published as real case study.
