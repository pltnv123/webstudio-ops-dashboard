# V6.6 Testimonial approval policy

Testimonials are allowed only when all conditions are true:

1. Exact quote is provided or explicitly approved by the client.
2. Client identity/public attribution is approved.
3. Date/source of approval is recorded.
4. Quote does not contain unsupported medical/legal/financial claims.
5. Owner approves publication.

Forbidden: synthetic praise, composite personas, anonymous fake testimonials, edited quotes without client approval.
