# Compliance Review Checklist — WebStudio V5.0

## Before public use
- Confirm industry category: general, health/wellness, medical, legal, finance, children, regulated marketplace, or other.
- Review all claims for evidence and approvals.
- Remove diagnosis, cure, guarantee, legal advice, financial promise, or outcome assurance language unless reviewed and approved.
- Verify testimonial consent and attribution rules.
- Verify image rights and model/location permission.
- Verify privacy: no private addresses, phones, emails, credentials, internal notes, or personal data unless explicitly intended and approved.
- Confirm public contact/booking destination and data handling.

## Approval result labels
- `APPROVED_FOR_DEMO`: safe in sanitized demo.
- `NEEDS_OWNER`: owner decision required.
- `CLIENT_REQUIRED`: client/source approval required.
- `BLOCKED_FOR_PUBLIC`: do not publish publicly.
