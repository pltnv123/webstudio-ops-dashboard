# Asset Intake Design — WebStudio V5.0

Status: READY_FOR_DEMO

## Goal
Create a repeatable, client-safe intake pack that separates:
- assets needed for a real project;
- proof/testimonials that may be used only with approval;
- compliance and review gates;
- missing content;
- safe demo/public placeholders;
- owner/client approval requirements before public use.

## Safety model
- Static snapshot only.
- No asset upload.
- No form submission.
- No real private client data.
- No CRM/email/Telegram/payment writes.
- No browser-side secrets.
- No fake proof, fake testimonials, fake logos, or unapproved claims.
- Medical/legal/financial wording needs specialist/client review before public use.

## Route
`/asset-intake-pack/`

The route shows a copyable client request template, checklist sections, missing-content tracker, and approval gates.
