# Asset Checklist — WebStudio V5.0

## Required assets
- Logo / wordmark / favicon, or approval to create a placeholder concept.
- Brand colors and fonts, if available.
- Real service descriptions and priority offers.
- Contact/booking preference: email, phone, booking link, messenger, or manual handoff.
- Legal/compliance notes for regulated categories.

## Optional assets
- Team/headshot photos.
- Location, product, workspace, process, or before/after photos.
- Existing brochures, menus, PDFs, decks, or brand guides.
- FAQ answers and objection-handling notes.
- Analytics, existing site screenshots, or SEO/search notes.

## Approval-gated assets
- Testimonials, reviews, ratings, logos, case studies, metrics, before/after claims.
- Any patient/client/customer identity.
- Any health, legal, financial, safety, or outcome claim.
- Public pricing/timeline promises.

## Safe demo use
- Generic placeholders.
- Sanitized descriptions.
- Generated/concept visuals clearly labeled as concept/demo.
- Artifact proof: checklists, QA reports, route smoke, build reports.
