# Proof Policy — WebStudio V5.0

## Allowed without extra proof
- Process artifacts: sitemap, page briefs, QA checklist, build/smoke reports.
- Demo-only screenshots of sanitized prototypes.
- Owner-approved internal status labels.

## Allowed only with owner/client approval
- Testimonials and reviews.
- Client logos and names.
- Case studies and quantified results.
- Before/after screenshots.
- Claims about revenue, conversion, bookings, health outcomes, legal outcomes, or financial outcomes.

## Not allowed
- Fake testimonials.
- Fake logos.
- Invented numbers.
- Unverified social proof.
- Unapproved health/legal/financial claims.
- Sensitive personal data in public demos.

## Rule
If proof is not approved and traceable, use artifact proof instead.
