# Client Data Policy v7.1

- Public demo routes must contain sanitized examples only.
- No private client names, personal contacts, contracts, files, credentials, internal notes, or negotiation data in the browser bundle.
- Real photos, testimonials, proof, case studies, logos, metrics, certifications, and regulated claims require source and approval.
- Missing inputs stay labeled as placeholder/TODO; do not invent proof.
- Live CRM/Telegram/email/payment/Supabase writes require separate owner approval and backend-only secret storage.
