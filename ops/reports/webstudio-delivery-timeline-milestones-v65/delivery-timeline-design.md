# Delivery Timeline Design — V6.5

Goal: show a client-safe delivery path from discovery to post-handoff follow-up without implying automation or live external writes.

Layout:
- Hero with marker `delivery-timeline-v65`.
- Safety contract proof grid.
- Milestone chain with numbered stages and status chips.
- Blockers / owner actions panel.
- Linked delivery routes panel.
- Collapsible sanitized JSON export.

Tone: operational, explicit, safe. The tracker is a demo/read-only planning view, not a live dispatcher.
